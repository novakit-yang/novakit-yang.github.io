Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NovaKit ("we", "us") handles information when you visit our website or purchase our products.

1. Information We Collect

  • Order information — your name, email address and purchase details, provided by our payment provider when you buy a product.
  • Payment data — handled entirely by our payment provider. We never see or store your full card details.
  • Support emails — if you contact us, we keep the email correspondence to handle your request.

2. How We Use Information

  • To deliver your purchased files and license keys.
  • To provide customer support and handle refunds.
  • To send essential order-related emails (receipts, download links).
  • We do not sell your personal data to anyone.

3. Cookies & Analytics

This website does not use tracking cookies. Our payment provider may set cookies on its checkout page for security and fraud prevention, governed by its own privacy policy.

4. Data Sharing

We share data only with our payment provider, to the extent necessary to process payments and comply with tax and legal obligations.

5. Data Retention

Order records are kept as long as required for accounting and legal purposes. Support emails are deleted after your issue is resolved, unless retention is required by law.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing us. You may also opt out of any non-essential emails via the unsubscribe link.
