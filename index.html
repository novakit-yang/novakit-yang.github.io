Questions

Before you buy

Do I need REAPER 7?

Yes — the project is saved in the REAPER 7 format. Any later version opens it without conversion. REAPER is sold separately by Cockos; the evaluation licence is fully functional, so you can try the template before you buy a licence.

Will it work in another DAW?

No. This is a REAPER project file, not a set of audio files or presets — its value is the routing, the stock plug-in chains and the render settings, all of which are REAPER-specific. The free written guide, however, applies to any DAW.

What if I have more than four speakers?

Select any guest track, duplicate it, and the new track inherits the full chain and stays inside the DIALOGUE BUS. There is no track limit — but each person needs their own recording.

Is audio included?

No. The MUSIC and SFX tracks are empty on purpose, so you can drop in your own royalty-cleared audio. No music, intros or sound effects are bundled.
